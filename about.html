Our Story

Baked with
love, art &
a pinch of magic

Frost & Fable began in a tiny home kitchen with one piping bag, a big dream, and a deep belief that dessert should tell a story. Today, we're a full atelier crafting edible art for life's sweetest moments.

See Our Work Say Hello
The Journey

From kitchen to atelier

2018

The first batch

Sarah baked 24 snowflake cookies for a friend's winter wedding. They sold out in minutes — and Frost & Fable was born.

2020

The atelier opens

We moved into our first commercial kitchen in the Frosting District. Classes began with just 4 students per session.

2022

10,000 cookies

We hit a huge milestone — 10,000 hand-decorated cookies. We celebrated with a giant cookie cake (obviously).

Today

A growing family

A team of 8 decorators, weekly classes, custom orders shipping nationwide — and still baking every cookie by hand.
