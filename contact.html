Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

How quickly do you respond?
We aim to respond within 2–4 hours during business hours. Emails sent after 6pm or on weekends will be answered the next business day.
Can I visit without an appointment?
Absolutely! Our atelier is open to walk-ins during regular hours. Come taste samples, browse our cookies, and chat with our team. For custom orders, we recommend booking a consultation.
Do you ship nationwide?
Yes! We ship cookies nationwide via priority mail. Local pickup and delivery are also available in the Sweetville area. Contact us for shipping rates.
How far in advance should I order?
For custom orders, 2–3 weeks notice is ideal. For large events or intricate designs, 4–6 weeks. Need something sooner? Reach out — we'll see what we can do.
Do you offer corporate invoicing?
Yes! We offer net-30 invoicing for corporate orders over $200. Get in touch and we'll set you up with a business account.
