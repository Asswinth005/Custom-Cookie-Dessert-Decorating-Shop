FAQ

Common questions

How far in advance should I order?
For custom orders, we recommend 2–3 weeks notice. For large events (100+ cookies) or intricate designs, 4–6 weeks is ideal. Need something sooner? Reach out — we'll see what we can do.
What's your minimum order?
Our custom orders start at 12 cookies. For corporate branding, the minimum is 24. There's no maximum — we've done orders of 1,000+ cookies!
Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Absolutely! We offer gluten-free, nut-free, and vegan options. Please note that our kitchen does handle nuts and wheat, so we can't guarantee zero cross-contamination.
How do you ship cookies?
We ship nationwide via priority mail in specially designed boxes with protective padding. Cookies are individually wrapped and arrive within 2–4 business days. Local pickup is also available.
Can I taste before I order?
Yes! We offer tasting boxes with 6 sample flavors for $18. If you place a custom order afterward, we'll credit the tasting box cost toward your order.
